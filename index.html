About the coach

Guidance that comes from lived experience

Sangeeta helps people work toward weight loss, healthy weight gain and an overall better lifestyle — without extreme rules. Her approach centres on ghar ka poshtik bhojan (home-cooked, nutritious food), correct nutrition, stress management, and small habits that are easy to actually keep up.

The core belief is simple: sustainable health results come from real food and real habits — not from starving, not from giving up the meals you love, and not from punishing workouts. It's about building a body that stays fit for life, not just for a season.
